What You Need To Know: Volleyball



🏐 Basic Rules
  • There can only be 6 Players in one side of the court
  • Each team can only have 3 touches
  • If the ball lands on a team's side of the court, the other team wins a point
🏐 Fundamental Skills
  • Receiving: passing the ball to your teammate or the other team
  • Serving: putting the ball into play at the start of a rally
  • Spiking: when a player jumps into the air to perform an attack to the opposing team
🏐 How to Play
  • A team serves to start the rally (passing the ball back and forth)
  • Players pass the ball to their teammates or the other team
  • The rally ends / the other team gains a point when the ball touches your side of the court
